What changed in v2, and why

The lamps are gone. You asked whether they earn their place. They don't, and the database says so twice over: 74.5% of utterances are confirmed, so the column would show the same green in three rows out of four — and there are seven statuses in the table, not three (ready, dispatching, dispatched_unconfirmed, dispatch_failed, transcription_failed, confirmed, discarded), which no three-colour lamp can represent honestly. A lamp lit on every row is decoration, and an annunciator that is lit at rest has stopped being an annunciator. Status now appears only when the row is not the ordinary case, as a line under the transcript, in the word that is actually true.

Actions are engraved words now. Your complaint — it isn't obvious what the buttons do — has a root cause: they were icons. A MOCR console labelled its controls with words, and an icon that needs a tooltip to explain itself has already failed the placard test. Play · Copy · Retry needs no legend. Tooltips are still there, but they carry the detail now rather than the meaning.

Send became Copy
You're right, and it's a bigger improvement than a swap. Re-dispatching types into a live terminal — the one action in this app that cannot be undone — and the session it was addressed to has usually moved on by the time you are looking at this screen. That is exactly the 20:53 case: it was queued behind a running turn, and re-sending it later would have landed in a conversation that had gone somewhere else. Copy is non-destructive, always correct, and it is already this app's established answer to undeliverable words: the dispatch path puts them on the clipboard rather than into the void. Settings should not be able to type into your sessions at all.

The empty row is drawn. It is real: four utterances have no transcript, every one transcription_failed with "no speech detected" — a gesture that closed before you spoke. The row leads with the fact (no transcript — 0.6s recording) instead of showing a blank, keeps Play so you can hear what was captured, hatches out Copy because there is nothing to copy, and says plainly that a retry will probably fail the same way. The raw provider error is a wall of kAFAssistantErrorDomain noise, so it collapses to "no speech detected · all three providers" with the full chain on hover.

Still standing from v1

  • Tabs Kept, as you said.
  • Calm at rest Actions stay invisible until a row is selected. Six rows of permanent buttons would make a list of your own words look like a control panel.
  • Past the horizon Recordings are reaped at 72 hours and the transcript outlives them, so that row keeps its text and its Copy and hatches out the two actions that need a wav — better than a button that fails.
  • No bold, anywhere Hierarchy is size and intensity only.
